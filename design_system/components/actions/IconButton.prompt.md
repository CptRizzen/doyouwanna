Circular/square icon-only tap target — nav bars, toolbars, map controls, close buttons.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="bell" tone="soft" label="Notifications" />
<IconButton icon="plus" tone="solid" label="New plan" />
<IconButton icon="x" tone="glass" label="Close" />   {/* over photos */}
```

Tones: `plain | soft | solid | glass | dark`. Shapes: `circle` (default) | `square`. Keep `size` ≥ 44 for touch.
